## Why we are watching before the draft

Our goal is to discover measurable player development **before the 2027 MLB Draft and first Bowman card release/repricing**. This is a **PRE-CARD RESEARCH** list, not a list of buyable Bowman 1st autos. Existing USA Baseball national-team selections are highly publicized and MUST NOT be described as undiscovered sleepers.

- Verified cheap 1st Bowman autos: **0** — not researched/available as an actionable card class.

**Data limit:** Partial PUBLIC tournament-roster and event-max-velocity snapshots, not a comprehensive national high-school database. No free DiamondKast access, hitting game logs, pitching command, spin, or replicated pitch-quality evidence. Showcase and public top-velocity leaderboard selection bias against unknown players. A 3+ mph jump between event PEAKS may be noise, environment, measurement changes or small sample; it does NOT establish a future skill breakout. Event name joins are not verified persistent identities; no Bowman card market claims or purchase recommendations.

## Source and model integrity

- The player list comes from **official public USA Baseball and publicly exposed tournament rosters**, not draft-prospect articles or rankings.
- The event fastball values are **single reported event maxima** (not a real season or multiple-start velocity average).
- A name on a velocity table without a confirmed grad year is **not** silently assigned to the 2027 draft class.
- A high-school graduate may attend college instead of being drafted/signed. A grad year is an eligibility-screen input, not a draft-outcome forecast.
- New live games and measured skills must establish repeated improvement before we classify a genuine breakout.
- This job does **not** create paper-card purchases or alter the existing $100 ledger.
