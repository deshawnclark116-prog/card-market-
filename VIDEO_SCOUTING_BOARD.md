## Evidence limits and safeguards

- Video clips must be source-authenticated, legally authorized for analysis, manually athlete-verified, SHA256-verified and timestamp-annotated.
- The engine decodes frames, uses a pose landmark model to calculate 2D screen angles and between-repetition variation, and rejects low resolution, poor-sharpness, missing-pose or unmatched-camera studies.
- No face identification, identity matching from images, automatic pitch classification, release-point inference or injury prediction.
- No pixel/video storage in GitHub outputs. Downloaded clips exist only in a temporary work directory during processing.
- Baseball value must be validated against future game performance before visual measurements contribute to probabilities or card-buy decisions.
- The 2027 high-school and 2026 MiLB statistics boards continue unchanged; the $100 paper ledger remains separate.
